## 레슨 1. Requesting an Escalation

| 항목 | English |
|---|---|
| 제목 | Requesting an Escalation |
| 짧은 이름 | Escalation |
| 부제 | When it's beyond your authority, pass it on so the customer doesn't have to repeat themselves. |
| 누가 | Agent → SME → TL |
| 노드 | Try it yourself · Tell the customer · Ask in one go · Record and follow through |

스텝:

1. This procedure exists to pass a contact you can't resolve to the SME quickly, without making the customer tell the story again.
2. Jimin takes a call from an angry customer asking for compensation. The first step is trying it yourself. Listen to the end and try first within the standard process and your own authority.
3. The second step is telling the customer. If it's beyond your authority, don't argue; say you'll pass it to the right person and how long they'll wait.
4. The third step is asking in one go. By messenger or by raising a hand, give Harin the customer's request, what you've already tried and how the customer is doing, all at once.
5. If you only ask "Can I give compensation?", Harin has to ask you back, and the customer waits all that time.
6. The fourth step is recording and following through. Mark the ticket as an escalation and record what the customer said and how they felt, as fact. When Harin answers, carry the result back to the customer.
7. While you wait for Harin's answer, tell the customer roughly how long it will take, and get back to them within the time you promised. After you give the result, check whether they have any other questions before you close.
8. A word from Miso: I thought my job was done once I'd escalated. On the days I left a detailed record, the customer got less angry and I got called over less.

## 레슨 2. Handling Abusive Callers and Ending the Call

| 항목 | English |
|---|---|
| 제목 | Handling Abusive Callers and Ending the Call |
| 짧은 이름 | Abusive callers |
| 부제 | Don't just endure it; end the call in the order your center sets. |
| 누가 | Agent → SME or TL |
| 노드 | Warn · Get approval · Announce the end · Record |

스텝:

1. This procedure exists so that when abuse and swearing go on, the agent doesn't endure it alone but ends the call in the order the center sets and leaves a record.
2. A customer on Jimin's call starts swearing. Being angry and swearing are different. Listen to an angry customer to the end, but follow the set procedure for swearing.
3. The first step is warning. Using the center's set wording, calmly say you can't continue helping if the swearing goes on.
4. Past the set number of times (Example: 3), move to the next step. The count and the wording differ by center, so know your center's rules ahead of time.
5. The second step is getting approval. If your center requires a supervisor's approval, get it from Harin or Sujin by voice or message before ending the call.
6. The third step is announcing the end. Say you are ending the call because you can't continue due to the swearing, then hang up. Don't hang up without a word.
7. The fourth step is recording. In the contact record, note how many warnings you gave, who approved it, and that the call ended due to abuse. If it was hard on you afterward, it's fine to tell Harin or Sujin you need a short break.
8. A word from Miso: I thought enduring the swearing to the end was being professional. I learned late that ending the call by the procedure is part of the job, and that the record protects the next agent.

## 레슨 3. Reporting an Absence or Sick Day

| 항목 | English |
|---|---|
| 제목 | Reporting an Absence or Sick Day |
| 짧은 이름 | Absence |
| 부제 | Tell them early, by the set method, to the set person. |
| 누가 | Agent → TL (SME if away) |
| 노드 | Tell them early · Set method · Right person · Send documents |

스텝:

1. This procedure exists so that when you can't come in because you're sick or something can't be avoided, you report it early and by the set method, so the team can fill the gap.
2. Jimin wakes up with a fever and feels she can't go in. When you're sick, rest. But if you report late or the wrong way, a day off can be recorded as a no-show.
3. The first step is telling them early. Report it 1 to 2 hours before your shift (Example). The team can use that time to fill the seat.
4. The second step is the set method. Report it the way your team or center has set: a call, a message or the attendance system. Don't ask a colleague to pass it on.
5. The third step is the right person. Tell Sujin directly, or Harin if Sujin is on leave or sick herself.
6. Keep the reason short and clear. If you're sick, say "I'm unwell and can't come in today," and add that you'll see a doctor and send a medical certificate. For an unexpected family or personal matter, give a rough reason and ask for Emergency Leave.
7. The fourth step is sending documents. After reporting sick and visiting a doctor, send a document such as a medical certificate to Sujin or Harin through the set channel on the same day. Deadlines can differ by center, so learn your center's rules ahead of time, including that sick days stay on your attendance record.
8. A word from Miso: On a sick day I once asked a colleague to "tell the TL," and it was recorded as a no-show. The sicker you are, the more reporting it yourself by the set method protects you.

## 레슨 4. Applying a Client Update from the Huddle

| 항목 | English |
|---|---|
| 제목 | Applying a Client Update from the Huddle |
| 짧은 이름 | Huddle |
| 부제 | Hear what changed, and guide customers the new way from the very first one. |
| 누가 | SME → Agent |
| 노드 | Listen and note · Ask if unsure · Apply right away · Read it again |

스텝:

1. This procedure exists so that when a client's policy or promotion changes, you guide customers the new way from the very first one after the huddle.
2. In a short huddle before the shift (Example: 5 minutes), Harin says the refund period changes from tomorrow. The first step is listening and noting. Write down what changes, from when, and for whom.
3. The second step is asking if unsure. Ask about anything confusing right there in the huddle. If Harin can't answer on the spot, she'll check and let you know.
4. If you guess on your own and it's wrong, the same mistake reaches many customers.
5. The third step is applying it right away. From the start time, guide customers with the wording Harin gave. Even if the old way is a habit, stick a note about the change next to your screen.
6. The fourth step is reading it again. If you were late for the huddle or off that day, read what was posted in the team chat before starting work.
7. During your shift, keep your notes beside your screen and check them on every inquiry the change applies to. For the first few days (Example), after each one, check yourself that you explained it the new way. Ask Harin about anything that confused you that day, and sort it out before your next shift.
8. A word from Miso: In huddles I just nodded and didn't write anything down. Only after scrubbing showed I'd quoted the old period that afternoon did I start taking notes.

## 레슨 5. Reporting a Knowledge Base Error

| 항목 | English |
|---|---|
| 제목 | Reporting a Knowledge Base Error |
| 짧은 이름 | KB errors |
| 부제 | If a document looks wrong, don't rewrite it for yourself; tell the person who can fix it. |
| 누가 | Agent → SME |
| 노드 | Notice something off · Check it · Report it · Follow the interim guidance |

스텝:

1. This procedure exists so that when you find something wrong or missing in the knowledge base, you report it in a way the person who fixes it can act on right away.
2. The first step is noticing something off. If a customer tells a different story after you followed the document, or the huddle and the document disagree, suspect the document.
3. The second step is checking it. Compare the document with the latest notice or the huddle to see if it really differs. If you're not sure, ask Harin first.
4. The third step is reporting it. Give Harin the document name, the sentence that looks wrong, and your basis (a notice or a ticket number) together.
5. If you only say "The refund document is weird," Harin has to go looking for it. Saying which sentence in which document gets it fixed fast.
6. The fourth step is following the interim guidance. Until it's fixed, handle contacts with the interim guidance Harin posted in the team chat. Don't guide customers differently on your own.
7. Mark the tickets you handled with the interim guidance. Once the knowledge base is fixed, read the change again and follow the fixed version from then on.
8. A word from Miso: When I saw a wrong document, I wrote the right answer in my own notes and used it alone. If I'd reported it, the whole team would have made that mistake less often.

| 보기 a (오답) | "The refund document seems a bit off." |
| a 피드백 | Without which sentence is off and why, Harin has to go looking again. |

## 레슨 6. Taking Scrubbing Feedback and Fixing It

| 항목 | English |
|---|---|
| 제목 | Taking Scrubbing Feedback and Fixing It |
| 짧은 이름 | Scrubbing feedback |
| 부제 | It's a cause, not a scolding; fix it so it doesn't happen again. |
| 누가 | SME → Agent |
| 노드 | Take the feedback · Understand the cause · Put it right · Stop it repeating |

스텝:

1. This procedure exists to understand the problem the SME found in your ticket during scrubbing, and to fix it so it doesn't happen again.
2. Harin finds that a verification step was skipped in one of Jimin's tickets and tells her briefly. The first step is taking the feedback. Before explaining yourself, confirm which part of which ticket it is.
3. The second step is understanding the cause. Find the cause yourself: did you skip it because you were busy, not know the step, or miss it on the screen?
4. Scrubbing isn't about finding who was wrong; it's about finding the cause. Harin doesn't post names in the team chat either.
5. The third step is putting it right. If the customer is still affected, decide with Harin how to fix it. For example, contact them again or complete the record.
6. The fourth step is stopping it repeating. Write the skipped step on a checklist beside your screen, and make sure to check it for the next few contacts.
7. After fixing it, check that the same note is gone from your next scrubbing results. If you're still unsure, ask Harin to walk you through a correct example once more.
8. A word from Miso: When I got scrubbing feedback, I felt scolded and got upset first. Once I saw it as someone finding the cause for me, the same mistakes dropped sharply.

| a 피드백 | You can explain later. First, confirm which part of which ticket it is. |
| 보기 b (정답) | Confirm which part of which ticket it is. |

## 레슨 7. Disputing a Quality Score

| 항목 | English |
|---|---|
| 제목 | Disputing a Quality Score |
| 짧은 이름 | Score dispute |
| 부제 | Ask with evidence, not feelings, through the set route. |
| 누가 | Agent → TL → QA |
| 노드 | Read the score sheet · Gather evidence · Ask the Team Leader · Accept the result |

스텝:

1. This procedure exists so that when a quality score item doesn't seem right, you gather evidence and ask for a re-review through the set route.
2. Jimin lost points on the guidance item in her score sheet, though she believes she guided the customer as the huddle said. The first step is reading the score sheet. Check which item lost points and by which standard.
3. The second step is gathering evidence. Collect the ticket number, the moment in the recording or record, and the notice or huddle content behind your guidance.
4. The third step is asking the TL. Don't argue with QA directly; within 24 to 48 hours (Example) of getting the score, ask for a re-review with your evidence through Sujin. The deadline can differ by center.
5. Sujin listens to the recording with you, and if there's a basis, asks QA for a re-review through the set procedure. The TL's QA support is what comes next.
6. The fourth step is accepting the result. Whether the score changes or not, hear the reason, and if the standard was different, guide by that standard from then on.
7. Cases like this are also used in calibration to align the standard. A dispute with evidence corrects the standard for the whole team, not just for you.
8. A word from Miso: When I got a score I thought was unfair, I just complained to colleagues. If I'd gathered evidence and asked once, properly, I'd have understood the standard much faster.

## 레슨 8. Responding to a System Outage

| 항목 | English |
|---|---|
| 제목 | Responding to a System Outage |
| 짧은 이름 | System outage |
| 부제 | Don't try to fix it alone; report it right away and be honest with customers. |
| 누가 | Agent → SME → TL |
| 노드 | Notice it · Report it right away · Tell the customer · Record and follow up |

스텝:

1. This procedure exists so that when the contact system or a client's system goes down, you don't hold on to it alone but report it right away, so the whole team responds the same way.
2. Order lookup keeps throwing an error on Jimin's screen. The first step is noticing it. Try the set first fixes (Example: refresh, log in again), and if it still fails, treat it as an outage.
3. The second step is reporting it right away. Report to Harin or Sujin which screen, since when and what error. They gather the errors and raise a ticket with the IT help desk to report it and get support.
4. If you hold on to it trying to fix it yourself, more agents keep getting stuck on the same error meanwhile.
5. The third step is telling the customer. With the wording Harin or Sujin gives, honestly say you can't check right now and explain how you'll get back to them. Don't promise a recovery time you're not sure of.
6. The fourth step is recording and following up. Mark the contacts you couldn't handle because of the outage as set, and once it's back, contact or handle them in order.
7. Once it's restored, read the recovery notice in the team chat first. Before working through the contacts you held, check with one case that the system really works.
8. A word from Miso: When an outage hit, I assumed it was my computer and just kept rebooting. If I'd reported it first, the whole team would have given the same guidance ten minutes sooner.

체크포인트:

| a 피드백 | While you hold on to it, more agents and customers get stuck. If the first fixes don't work, report it right away. |
