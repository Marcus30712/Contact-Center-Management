## 레슨 1. Supporting Agents

| 항목 | English |
|---|---|
| 제목 | Supporting Agents |
| 짧은 이름 | Agents |
| 부제 | Make expectations clear, listen regularly, and step in when they're stuck. |
| 노드 | Hand over · Get back · Help · Step in |

스텝:

1. This lesson looks at how a team leader supports agents in four stages: handing over, getting back, helping and stepping in. One agent's growth over time is covered by The Agent Lifecycle, so this lesson is about the support you give every week.
2. The first stage is handing over. Tell Jimin clearly what goals and role you expect of her. Without knowing what good looks like, an agent can't find their own way.
3. The second stage is getting back. What you get from agents isn't only numbers. Hear the floor's stories, such as what customers often ask and what makes work awkward, in regular one-on-ones (Example: at the weekly score review).
4. The third stage is helping. Give coaching, praise, and the tools and materials they need. For someone new like Seojun, stay closer together with the SME.
5. Strong performers need support too. Give long-serving people like Jimin chances to grow, such as new work or a buddy role.
6. The fourth stage is stepping in. When the same problem keeps coming back, or there is an attendance or conduct issue, the team leader steps in directly. If coaching doesn't fix a performance problem, follow the performance improvement plan (PIP); for attendance or conduct, follow the center's attendance rules and HR procedure.
7. The sooner you step in, the smaller it stays. Put it off for a few weeks and a small habit becomes a big problem.
8. You don't help every agent the same way. The personal notes you made in TL First Steps tell you who needs what.

## 레슨 2. Supporting the SME

| 항목 | English |
|---|---|
| 제목 | Supporting the SME |
| 짧은 이름 | SME |
| 부제 | Agree on the approval scope and the work to cover, and get the trends from the floor. |
| 노드 | Hand over · Get back · Help · Step in |

스텝:

1. This lesson looks at how a team leader supports the SME, from the team leader's side.
2. The first stage is handing over. Agree on the approval scope list with Harin, the SME, and decide what she will cover for you, such as attendance or huddles. A clear scope lets Harin decide without asking every time.
3. The second stage is getting back. Get the weekly trend summary, attendance results and each member's stage from Harin on agreed days. Use the summary straight away in that week's coaching plan.
4. The third stage is helping. SMEs tend to try to hold out alone when requests pile up. At busy times, the team leader helps support the floor or adjusts staffing with the RTA.
5. Help the SME grow too. If Harin wants to become a team leader, give her a little of that work at a time, such as running a meeting or planning for new hires.
6. The fourth stage is stepping in. Matters the SME passes up, such as attendance, performance actions and approvals outside policy, are decided by the team leader. Tell Harin the outcome of approval calls so she knows what to do if it happens again, and keep attendance or performance actions between you, the agent and only those who must know, such as your assistant manager or HR.
7. Don't use the SME as a mini team leader. Handing over evaluation and action breaks the relationship between the SME and the agents.

| 보기 a (오답) | Answer each one right away. |
| a 피드백 | Answering every time blocks your day, and Harin never gets a standard to judge by herself. |
| 보기 b (정답) | Go over the approval scope list with Harin again. |

| 보기 c (오답) | It's Harin's report, so act right away without checking. |
| c 피드백 | The team leader has to check the facts directly for the action to be fair. |
| 해설 | Matters passed up to you are checked and decided by the team leader directly. |

## 레슨 3. Supporting QA

| 항목 | English |
|---|---|
| 제목 | Supporting QA |
| 짧은 이름 | QA |
| 부제 | Align on the scoring standard together, and turn quality results into coaching. |
| 노드 | Hand over · Get back · Help · Step in |

스텝:

1. This lesson looks at how a team leader works with QA to turn quality scoring into the team's growth.
2. The first stage is handing over. Ask Dohyun from QA to look closely at what matters for your team. For example, in the week a new policy starts, ask for more of those contacts to be scored.
3. The second stage is getting back. Get each member's quality score and the items that lose points most often on a set cycle (Example: weekly). To use it for coaching, get the reasons and recorded examples, not just the scores.
4. The third stage is helping. Join calibration and bring the floor's reality and the team leader's view. When a standard changes, tell the team first so no agent is scored on a standard they don't know.
5. If an agent disputes a score, the team leader first listens to the recording with them and asks for a re-review through the agreed process only when there are grounds. Don't leave QA and the agent to argue it out directly.
6. The fourth stage is stepping in. If the team's quality keeps falling, or people say scoring varies by evaluator, meet QA separately and look at the cause together.
7. QA isn't the other side but a colleague with the same goal. Listening to the same recording together changes more than arguing over scores.

|---|---|

| 보기 a (오답) | A team ranking (so you can see at once who is lowest) |
| a 피드백 | A ranking shows only who is lowest, not what to coach. |

| 보기 c (오답) | The names of the evaluators |

| 보기 a (오답) | It's his score, so tell Seojun to go to QA and argue it himself. |

| 보기 c (오답) | It's QA's scoring, so calm him down and tell him to accept it this time. |
| c 피드백 | Blocking a dispute that has evidence makes agents stop trusting the scoring. |
| 해설 | The team leader checks the grounds for a dispute first and passes it on through the process. |

## 레슨 4. Supporting Trainers

| 항목 | English |
|---|---|
| 제목 | Supporting Trainers |
| 짧은 이름 | Trainers |
| 부제 | Ask for the training the floor needs, and carry the results onto the floor. |
| 노드 | Hand over · Get back · Help · Step in |

스텝:

1. This lesson looks at how a team leader works with trainers to shape new-hire and refresher training to fit the floor.
2. The first stage is handing over. Ask Yuna the trainer for the training your team needs, with evidence. Attaching cases, such as "12 wrong explanations on refund contacts this month (Example)," makes the training goal clear.
3. The second stage is getting back. Get new hires' training records, the types to watch in nesting and graduation assessments from Yuna, and share them with the SME.
4. The third stage is helping. Tell her when training material and the floor's way differ, and find real cases and recordings for training. Cases from the floor keep training alive.
5. What happens after training matters more. Check with the SME whether what was learned is used on the floor, and tell Yuna the results.
6. The fourth stage is stepping in. If a new hire isn't ready, decide with Yuna to hold back graduation, and whether to extend nesting or add extra training. Don't force someone onto the floor because you're short of people.
7. The trainer is responsible for the classroom, the team leader for the floor. New hires stay longer when both see them through the same eyes.

체크포인트:

| 항목 | English |
|---|---|
| 1번 질문 | What fits when you ask a trainer for extra training? |
| 보기 a (오답) | "The team seems weak across the board lately. Could you run the basics again?" |
| a 피드백 | Without knowing what is weak, the trainer can't set a training goal. |
| 보기 b (정답) | "There were 12 wrong refund explanations this month (Example). Cases are attached." |
| 보기 c (오답) | "Please add more to the next class's training, so new hires don't hit the same problem." |
| c 피드백 | Training the next class won't fix the problems of the people on your team now. |
| 해설 | Ask for training with cases that show what needs learning and why. |
| 2번 질문 | You're short of people, and a new hire about to graduate from nesting isn't ready yet. What do you do? |
| 보기 a (오답) | You need people, so let them graduate on schedule and put them on the regular team right away. |
| a 피드백 | Putting someone on the floor before they're ready is hard on both customers and the new hire, who may well leave. |
| 보기 b (정답) | Decide with the trainer to hold back graduation, and choose between longer nesting and extra training. |
| 보기 c (오답) | Give the new hire materials and tell them to study more on their own before graduation. |
| c 피드백 | Left alone, they keep getting stuck in the same places, and time passes without knowing what is missing. |
| 해설 | Get a new hire who isn't ready prepared with the trainer before putting them on the floor. |

## 레슨 5. Supporting the Whole Team

| 항목 | English |
|---|---|
| 제목 | Supporting the Whole Team |
| 짧은 이름 | Whole team |
| 부제 | Use huddles and team meetings to keep everyone on the same goal, and connect the roles. |
| 노드 | Hand over · Get back · Help · Step in |

스텝:

1. This lesson looks at how a team leader connects agents, the SME, QA and trainers so they work as one team toward the same goal instead of separately.
2. The first stage is handing over. Set the team goal in words everyone understands, and show how each role contributes to it.
3. The second stage is getting back. At regular team meetings (Example: once a week), have the SME, QA and the trainer share what they've seen. Connections you miss when you hear them separately show up in one room.
4. For example, Harin says refund contacts are up, Dohyun says points lost on refund explanations are up, and Yuna says the refund training is based on the old policy. The three stories point to one cause.
5. The third stage is helping. At the daily huddle, briefly share today's priorities and policy changes, and praise specific people for specific work.
6. The fourth stage is stepping in. When roles clash, for example an agent and QA disagreeing over a score, the team leader hears both sides and brings everyone back to one standard through calibration with QA.
7. Report the team's results to your assistant manager on a set cycle, and turn the direction you get back into team goals. The team leader is the link between above and below.
8. After one round like this, it's time to look at one person at a time again. What you saw across the team is where the next round of agent support begins.

|---|---|
| 1번 질문 | The SME, QA and the trainer each raise a different problem. What should the team leader do first? |

| a 피드백 | Solving them separately means fixing the same cause three different ways. |

| c 피드백 | If the problems are connected, fixing just one lets them come back. |
| 해설 | Connecting the roles is the team leader's job. |
| 2번 질문 | What fits when giving praise at the huddle? |
| 보기 a (오답) | "Thanks for your hard work, everyone. We beat the target again this week, so let's keep it up today!" |
| a 피드백 | If no one knows who did what well, no one knows what to keep doing. |
| 보기 b (정답) | "Yesterday Jimin saw a tough refund case through to the end and got a thank-you email." |
| 보기 c (오답) | Give praise only in one-on-ones, so other team members don't feel compared. |
| c 피드백 | Praise in front of the team shows everyone what good work looks like. Correct in private; praise together. |
| 해설 | Give praise specifically, in front of the team. |
