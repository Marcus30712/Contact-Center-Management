## 퀴즈 1. Team lead review quiz

### 문제 1

| 항목 | English |
|---|---|
| 질문 | Jimin, now on the regular team, has an average handle time (AHT) longer than the team average. His FCR and QA accuracy are high. Your assistant manager tells you to bring the AHT down. What do you do first? |
| 보기 a (오답) | Start a performance improvement plan (PIP) right away, with the team-average AHT as the goal. |
| a 피드백 | A PIP is the formal procedure for when coaching is not enough. Starting it over one number also shakes what Jimin does well: solving things in one go. |
| 보기 b (정답) | Look at a few recordings together with the metrics, and in a one-on-one find what he does well and which parts can be shorter. |
| 보기 c (오답) | Announce in the team huddle that calls need to end faster. |
| c 피드백 | Stressing time alone teaches agents to hang up fast rather than to explain correctly. |
| 해설 | Looking at one metric leads to coaching that sacrifices the others. Look at several metrics and real contacts together, and agree on behavior in a one-on-one. |
| 관련 레슨 | Lifecycle lesson 4 · Performance Management and Coaching (../agent-lifecycle/#coaching-0) · SOP · One-on-One Coaching (../sop-library/#coaching-0) |

### 문제 2

| 항목 | English |
|---|---|
| 질문 | You have coached Jimin one on one three times, but he keeps skipping the same check step. What comes next? |
| 보기 a (오답) | Coach him once more on the same thing. |
| a 피드백 | If the same approach failed three times, it is time to set goals and support formally. |
| 보기 b (오답) | Suggest he leave, since there is no sign of improvement. |
| b 피드백 | Ending it without a chance or support tells the team that asking for help is pointless. |
| 보기 c (정답) | Move to a PIP: meet with the data, agree on goals and the support you will give, and check in weekly. |
| 해설 | When coaching alone is not enough, move to a PIP. A PIP is not a punishment but a procedure that makes goals, support and check-ins clear. |
| 관련 레슨 | SOP · Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) (../sop-library/#pip-0) · SOP · One-on-One Coaching (../sop-library/#coaching-0) |

### 문제 3

| 항목 | English |
|---|---|
| 질문 | A promotion error sets off a flood of identical complaint calls, and an agent who just ended an abusive call sits frozen at the desk. What fits best as the first thing to do? |
| 보기 a (정답) | Let that agent take a short break, and tell the OM, QA and WFM right away that the same complaint is piling up. |
| 보기 b (오답) | Send the agent straight back to the next call, since many customers are waiting. |
| b 피드백 | The shock carries straight into the next customer, and the agent feels unprotected. |
| 보기 c (오답) | Handle it inside the team until the complaints calm down. |
| c 피드백 | The first step in a large-scale issue is spotting and sharing it. Holding it alone delays cause analysis and staffing plans. |
| 해설 | Protect the person first, and raise the surge right away so several teams can move together. |
| 관련 레슨 | Lifecycle lesson 5 · Engagement and Retention (../agent-lifecycle/#retention-0) · SOP · Large-Scale Issue Response (../sop-library/#incident-0) |

### 문제 4

| 항목 | English |
|---|---|
| 질문 | For the second time, an exit interview brings up "I didn't know shifts would change this often." Next week you sit in on hiring interviews. What do you do? |
| 보기 a (오답) | Explain shift work only to those who ask, so applicant numbers don't drop. |
| a 피드백 | People who heard only the good parts start thinking of leaving the day they get their first rota. |
| 보기 b (정답) | Share the interview findings with the recruiter, and explain shift work honestly in the interviews. |
| 보기 c (오답) | Treat it as the leaver's personal situation and just keep the interview notes. |
| c 피드백 | When the same words keep coming up, it is not a personal matter but a sign the job preview needs fixing. |
| 해설 | Improvements from exit interviews feed the next round of hiring. A realistic job preview (RJP) cuts early leaving. |
| 관련 레슨 | Lifecycle lesson 6 · Career Development and Offboarding (../agent-lifecycle/#career-0) · Lifecycle lesson 1 · Recruitment and Hiring (../agent-lifecycle/#hiring-0) |

### 문제 5

| 항목 | English |
|---|---|
| 질문 | Two weeks (Example) into being team leader, Haeun, your closest colleague, complains that QA scores differ by evaluator. What fits best? |
| 보기 a (오답) | Ask QA to look at Haeun's scores again, since you are close. |
| a 피드백 | Looking after one person makes the team feel the standard depends on friendship. |
| 보기 b (오답) | Stay out of it, since evaluation is QA's job. |
| b 피드백 | Team leaders take part in calibration too. If the standard wobbles, so does the basis for coaching. |
| 보기 c (정답) | Treat it as a question about the standard: hold a calibration session with QA to align it, then tell the whole team the outcome. |
| 해설 | Find the team-wide standard problem behind one person's complaint, and settle it the same way for everyone. |

### 문제 6

| 항목 | English |
|---|---|
| 질문 | Seoa, a new hire in her first week, hardly asks any questions. While scrubbing, you see the same missing explanation twice in her tickets. What fits best? |
| 보기 a (정답) | Go to Seoa first with her buddy, ask where she gets stuck, and go over the missing explanation one on one. |
| 보기 b (오답) | Show Seoa's tickets as an example in the team huddle. |
| b 피드백 | Taking a new hire's mistakes in front of the team makes Seoa ask even less. |
| 보기 c (오답) | It is only the first week, so just watch for a month. |
| c 피드백 | The first weeks are when people are most likely to leave. Leaving her stuck for long makes it hard to hold on. |
| 해설 | Don't wait for new hires; reach out first, and go over mistakes one on one. A buddy closes that distance. |
| 관련 레슨 | SOP · New Hire Onboarding (../sop-library/#onboarding-0) · SOP · Scrubbing (../sop-library/#scrubbing-0) |

### 문제 1

| 항목 | English |
|---|---|
| 질문 | Seojun has a customer on hold and his hand up, your peer Junho asks for a compensation approval, and Sujin, in a meeting, messages you to take the afternoon attendance. What do you do first? |
| 보기 a (정답) | Go to Seojun first, and tell Junho and the team leader briefly when you will get to them. |
| 보기 b (오답) | Approve Junho's request first, since he is your peer. |

| 보기 c (오답) | Do the attendance first, since the team leader asked. |
| c 피드백 | Attendance can wait a moment; the customer on hold cannot. |
| 해설 | Customers waiting live come first, and whoever has to wait hears when you will come. |

| 항목 | English |

| 질문 | In your first week as SME, your peer Junho asks you to approve a large compensation that policy does not cover. The customer is waiting on the call. What do you do? |

| 해설 | You decide within your limits and pass on what is outside them. A peer's request follows the same standard. |
