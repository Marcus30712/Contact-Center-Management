## 퀴즈 1. TL review quiz

### 문제 1

| 항목 | English |
|---|---|
| 질문 | Jimin, now on the regular team, has an average handle time (AHT) longer than the team average. Her FCR and QA accuracy are high. Your assistant manager tells you to bring the AHT down. What do you do first? |
| 보기 a (오답) | Start a performance improvement plan (PIP) right away, with the team-average AHT as the goal. |
| a 피드백 | A PIP is the formal procedure for when coaching is not enough. Starting it over one number also shakes what Jimin does well: solving things in one go. |
| 보기 b (정답) | Review recordings with the metrics, and in a one-on-one find what she does well and which parts can be shorter. |
| 보기 c (오답) | Restate the AHT target at the team huddle and announce that calls need to end faster. |
| c 피드백 | Stressing time alone teaches agents to hang up fast rather than to explain correctly. |
| 해설 | Looking at one metric leads to coaching that sacrifices the others. Look at several metrics and real contacts together, and agree on behavior in a one-on-one. |
| 관련 레슨 | Lifecycle lesson 4 · Performance Management and Coaching (../agent-lifecycle/#coaching-0) · TL SOP · One-on-One Coaching (../sop-library/#coaching-0) |

### 문제 2

| 항목 | English |
|---|---|
| 질문 | You have coached Jimin one on one three times, but she keeps skipping the same check step. What comes next? |
| 보기 a (오답) | Keep the same approach and book one more one-on-one on the same point. |
| a 피드백 | If the same approach failed three times, it is time to set goals and support formally. |
| 보기 b (오답) | Suggest she leave, since three chances brought no sign of improvement. |
| b 피드백 | Ending it without formal goals and support tells the team that asking for help is pointless. |
| 보기 c (정답) | Move to a PIP: meet with the data, agree on goals and support, and check in weekly. |
| 해설 | When coaching alone is not enough, move to a PIP. A PIP is not a punishment but a procedure that makes goals, support and check-ins clear. |
| 관련 레슨 | TL SOP · Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) (../sop-library/#pip-0) · TL SOP · One-on-One Coaching (../sop-library/#coaching-0) |

### 문제 3

| 항목 | English |
|---|---|
| 질문 | A promotion error sets off a flood of identical complaint calls, and an agent who just ended an abusive call sits frozen at the desk. What fits best as the first thing to do? |
| 보기 a (정답) | Let the agent take a short break, and report the surge to your assistant manager or operation manager right away. |
| 보기 b (오답) | Worried about the waiting customers, reassure the agent, send them straight back to calls and talk after the shift. |
| b 피드백 | The shock carries straight into the next customer, and the agent feels unprotected. |
| 보기 c (오답) | Agree on a response script within the team and handle it there until the complaints calm down. |
| c 피드백 | The first step in a large-scale issue is spotting and sharing it. Holding it alone delays cause analysis and staffing plans. |
| 해설 | Protect the person first, and report the surge to your assistant manager or operation manager right away so several teams can move together. |
| 관련 레슨 | Lifecycle lesson 5 · Engagement and Retention (../agent-lifecycle/#retention-0) · TL SOP · Large-Scale Issue Response (../sop-library/#incident-0) |

### 문제 4

| 항목 | English |
|---|---|
| 질문 | For the second time, an exit interview brings up "I didn't know shifts would change this often." Next week you sit in on hiring interviews. What do you do? |
| 보기 a (오답) | Explain shift work briefly, and only to applicants who ask, so applicant numbers don't drop. |
| a 피드백 | People who heard only the good parts start thinking of leaving the day they get their first schedule. |
| 보기 b (정답) | Share it with the recruiter, and explain shift work honestly in the interviews. |
| 보기 c (오답) | It's only two people, so put it down to their personal situations and just keep the interview notes. |
| c 피드백 | When the same words keep coming up, it is not a personal matter but a sign the job preview needs fixing. |
| 해설 | Improvements from exit interviews feed the next round of hiring. A realistic job preview (RJP) cuts early leaving. |
| 관련 레슨 | Lifecycle lesson 6 · Career Development and Offboarding (../agent-lifecycle/#career-0) · Lifecycle lesson 1 · Recruitment and Hiring (../agent-lifecycle/#hiring-0) |

### 문제 5

| 항목 | English |
|---|---|
| 질문 | Two weeks (Example) into being team leader, Haeun, your closest colleague, complains that QA scores differ by evaluator. What fits best? |
| 보기 a (오답) | Quietly ask QA to look at Haeun's scores again, trusting her account. |
| a 피드백 | Looking after one person makes the team feel the standard depends on friendship. |
| 보기 b (오답) | Stay out of it, since evaluation is QA's job. |
| b 피드백 | Team leaders take part in calibration too. If the standard wobbles, so does the basis for coaching. |
| 보기 c (정답) | It may be a team-wide problem, so ask QA for calibration and tell the team the outcome. |
| 해설 | Find the team-wide standard problem behind one person's complaint, and settle it the same way for everyone. |

### 문제 6

| 항목 | English |
|---|---|
| 질문 | Seoa, a new hire in her first week after Go Live, hardly asks any questions. While scrubbing, you see the same missing explanation twice in her tickets. What fits best? |
| 보기 a (정답) | Go to Seoa first with her buddy, ask where she's stuck, and go over the missing explanation one on one. |
| 보기 b (오답) | Show Seoa's tickets as an example at the team huddle to stop the mistake repeating. |
| b 피드백 | Taking a new hire's mistakes in front of the team makes Seoa ask even less. |
| 보기 c (오답) | Keeping her first week in mind, watch quietly for a month until she asks. |
| c 피드백 | Many people also leave in their first weeks on the floor. Leaving her stuck for long makes it hard to hold on. |
| 해설 | Don't wait for new hires; reach out first, and go over mistakes one on one. A buddy closes that distance. |
| 관련 레슨 | TL SOP · New Hire Onboarding (../sop-library/#onboarding-0) · TL SOP · Scrubbing (../sop-library/#scrubbing-0) |

## 퀴즈 2. SME review quiz

### 문제 1

| 항목 | English |
|---|---|
| 질문 | Seojun has a customer on hold and his hand up, your peer Junho asks by ticket for a compensation approval for a customer whose call has ended, and Sujin, in a meeting, messages you to take the afternoon attendance. What do you do first? |
| 보기 a (정답) | The customer on hold comes first, so go to Seojun, and tell Junho and the team leader when you will get to them. |
| 보기 b (오답) | Approve your peer Junho's request first; it's already in a ticket, so it's quick. |
| b 피드백 | The order is set by whether a customer is waiting now, not by what's quick or by friendship. |
| 보기 c (오답) | The team leader asked, so do attendance first and signal Seojun to wait a moment. |
| c 피드백 | Attendance can wait a moment; the customer on hold cannot. |
| 해설 | Customers waiting live come first, and whoever has to wait hears when you will come. |
| 관련 레슨 | SME First Steps lesson 6 · When Requests Pile Up at Once (../sme/#priority-0) · SME First Steps lesson 2 · Supporting Agents in Training or Just Trained (../sme/#newcomer-0) |

| 항목 | English |
|---|---|
| 질문 | In your first week as SME, your peer Junho asks you to approve a large compensation that policy does not cover. The customer is waiting on the call. What do you do? |
| 보기 a (오답) | Junho has more experience, so trust his call and approve it this once. |
| a 피드백 | A decision beyond your limits is hard to undo, and experience does not replace the standard. |

| b 피드백 | Turning it down leaves the customer's problem where it was. What you cannot decide goes to someone who can. |
| 보기 c (정답) | It is outside your limits, so ask Junho to tell the customer there is a short wait, and pass it to the team leader right away. |
| 해설 | You decide within your limits and pass on what is outside them. A peer's request follows the same standard. |
